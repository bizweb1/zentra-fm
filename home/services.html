Straight answers

What we do and don't take on.

Worth reading before you book. Nobody enjoys finding out on the day.

Always included, no extra charge

  • All equipment, cleaners, and cloths
  • Moving light furniture to clean underneath
  • Following your notes on products and problem spots
  • Text when we arrive and again when we lock up
  • A redo within 24 hours if something's missed
  • Working around pets who are comfortable with us

Not something we handle

  • Exterior or second-story windows
  • Carpet shampooing or steam extraction
  • Mold remediation and pest control
  • Biohazard, crime scene, or hoarding cleanup
  • Post-construction debris and drywall dust
  • Moving heavy furniture or appliances
  • Climbing above a two-step ladder

If your home needs something on the right-hand list, say so when you book. We'd rather point you to someone who does it properly than send a crew who can't finish the job.
