Good to know

Questions before you book.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients give us a lockbox code, a garage code, or a key. We text when we arrive and again when we lock up, and you can be there for the first visit if you'd rather meet the crew.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — equipment, cleaners, and cloths, all included. If you prefer we use specific products for allergies, pets, or stone surfaces, leave them out and tell us in your notes.

Why does the first clean cost more?

The initial deep clean handles built-up grime that recurring visits keep from coming back. It takes longer and usually more people. After that, your recurring rate is what you'll pay.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and redo it at no charge. That's the whole policy — no forms, no arguing about what was on the checklist.

Are you the same company as Zentra Facility Solutions?

Same company, same standards. Zentra Facility Solutions handles commercial, medical, and government buildings. Zentra Home is the residential side, with crews trained to the same inspection process.
