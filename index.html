FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

Recurring commercial janitorial service, deep cleaning, medical-grade disinfection, government facility contracts, property management cleaning, light facility maintenance, and residential cleaning as an add-on for our commercial clients.

How is pricing determined?

We do a free on-site walkthrough, review your square footage, traffic, and requirements, then quote a custom maintenance plan. Monthly contracts are available and we're flexible on terms to earn your business.

Are your teams vetted?

Yes — every Zentra team member is background-checked, and you get the same crew on every visit so they learn your building and your standards.

What products and protocols do you use?

Premium, eco-friendly supplies are included, and our disinfection process kills 99% of common bacteria and viruses — with compliance-minded protocols for medical and government facilities.

How do I schedule or change service?

Text, call, or email your dedicated account manager — or use the chat on this page — and changes are handled fast, usually same-day.
