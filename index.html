FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

Regular maintenance cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, specialized cleaning for kitchens, bathrooms, and high-traffic zones, plus light facility maintenance. Every plan can be customized to your needs.

How do you determine the cost of cleaning services?

Pricing depends on the size of the space, the type of service, and frequency. Residential cleans start at $99, and recurring plans get special locked-in rates. Request a free estimate and we'll give you an exact number.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No — many clients provide access instructions and come home to a spotless space. We do a pre and post walk-through with you whenever you'd like.

What products do you use?

Premium, eco-friendly supplies are included in every plan. Our disinfecting process kills 99% of common bacteria and viruses.

How do I schedule or change an appointment?

Message your dedicated account rep by text, call, email — or use the chat on this page — and we'll handle it right away.
